# Mixer Node

The **Mixer Node** is a 4-channel audio and signal mixer with independent Gain and Pan controls per channel, CV inputs for volume and panning automation, and a Master Gain output stage.

## Inputs

- **In 1 / In 2 / In 3 / In 4**: Audio/Signal inputs for channels 1 through 4.
- **Gain 1-4 CV**: CV modulation inputs for individual channel gain levels.
- **Pan 1-4 CV**: CV modulation inputs for individual channel stereo panning.

## Outputs

- **Audio Out**: Summed stereo master audio output.

## Controls

- **Ch 1-4 Gain**: Manual volume slider per channel (0.0 to 2.0, default 1.0).
- **Ch 1-4 Pan**: Stereo pan slider per channel (-1.0 Left to +1.0 Right, default 0.0).
- **Master Gain**: Overall master output gain slider (0.0 to 2.0, default 1.0).
