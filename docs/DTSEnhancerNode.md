# DTS Enhancer Node

The **DTS Enhancer Node** adds spatial depth, presence, dynamic stereo expansion, and subtle harmonic warmth to incoming audio signals.

## Inputs

- **Audio In**: The incoming audio signal to enhance.

## Outputs

- **Audio Out**: The enhanced stereo output signal.

## Controls

- **Enhance Depth**: Controls the intensity of the spatial enhancement and presence processing (0.0 to 1.0).
