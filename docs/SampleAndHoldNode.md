# Sample & Hold Node

The **Sample & Hold Node** samples an incoming voltage signal on each trigger pulse and holds that sampled voltage constant at the output until the next trigger.

## Inputs

- **Signal In**: The source audio or CV signal to be sampled.
- **Trigger**: The trigger or clock pulse that initiates a new sample capture.

## Outputs

- **Out**: The sampled stepped control voltage output.

## Controls

*None (purely event and signal driven).*
