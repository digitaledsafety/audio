# Sample & Hold Node

The **Sample & Hold Node** captures (samples) the instantaneous voltage level of an incoming signal whenever triggered, holding that sampled voltage constant until the next trigger.

## Inputs

- **Signal In**: Source audio or control signal to sample.
- **Trigger**: Incoming clock trigger or gate pulse that triggers voltage sampling.

## Outputs

- **CV Out**: Sampled and held constant DC control voltage level.

## Controls

*None (sampling occurs directly upon receiving trigger pulses).*
